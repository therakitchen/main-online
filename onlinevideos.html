FAQ
Choosing the right learning path is important when starting your formulation journey. Whether you prefer to focus on mastering one product at a time or explore a full range of related formulations, our modules are designed to give you flexibility, clarity, and practical skills.

Below are some of the most common questions to help you understand the difference between our Single Modules and Package Modules, so you can confidently choose what best fits your goals.
What is the difference between a Single Module and a Package Module?

A Single Module focuses on mastering one specific product, allowing you to learn the formulation in detail.
A Package Module includes a group of related products, helping you create a full set or collection within one program.

Which option is better for beginners?

A Single Module is ideal for beginners who want to focus, practice, and avoid overwhelm.
A Package Module is suitable if you’re ready to learn multiple related products at once.

Who should choose the Package Module?

The Package Module is ideal for those who want to create a complete and cohesive product line. It’s perfect for aspiring business owners, brand builders, or anyone who wants to learn multiple related formulations in one go while saving on overall cost.

Who should choose the Single Module?

The Single Module is best for individuals who prefer to focus on mastering one product at a time. It’s ideal for beginners, hobbyists, or those who want to test and refine a specific formulation before expanding into more products.

Can I purchase both Single and Package Modules?

Yes. You can purchase any module based on your needs. This is a one-time purchase system—no subscriptions and no upgrades required.
